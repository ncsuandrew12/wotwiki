
CHARACTER NAME DATASET NOTES

*Version History at End

***INTRO***

This dataset was originally created to track the names and their numbers. I later decided to add the extra data points (species, affiliation, nationality, etc.) since I figured they could be useful or interesting, especially for future analyses. With that in mind, the extra data is not perfect. I did my best, but I also wanted to complete this project in a month or less, which meant I couldn’t take the time to fully focus on the accuracy of the extra data. Gender and species should be accurate, but I gave less time to the others.

***GENERAL NOTES***

-This dataset started with all of the character entries in the WoT Companion. I then compared the data to various online resources.

-A fair amount of error checking has been done, but there is most likely some issues that still remain.

-I originally had a number of aliases and titles that were questionable, such as misspelled names, general titles, etc. I removed all of them since they didn’t feel like legitimate entries.

***GUIDE TO COLUMNS***

TYPE: The type of name it is. Includes “Alias” (another name used by a character), “Primary” (the primary name for a character), and “Title” (a title for a character such as The Dragon Reborn for Rand). For characters such as Padan Fain and the Forsaken I only used one primary character instead of treating their various incarnations as separate people.

BOOK: Which books they are found in. Can be “Main” (found in books 1-14), “NS” (found in New Spring, but not the main series), “World” (found in the World book, but not the main series or New Spring), or “WoTC” (only found in the Wheel of Time Companion).

FIRSTO: First Occurrence. Indicates which book contains the first occurrence of the name, which may or may not be the same as the character’s first appearance (since they may have appeared earlier without a name, or going by an alias). I tried to search both first and last name, but I forgot on some of them and just searched first name, so if they were introduced by their last name in an earlier book, I may have missed it. Note that I used publication order, so New Spring is treated as book #10.5 (between CoT and KoD).

APPEARS: Whether or not the characters makes a physical appearance in the series (1-14 + NS)

CA: Chapter Appearances. This is the number of chapters in which a character physically appears. For wolves, I only counted them if they were actually present, but not if they were communicating with Perrin telepathically from somewhere else. The data originally comes from the WoT Fandom Wiki, but I did a fair bit of error checking and updating while doing this project. The numbers may not be perfect, but they should be mostly accurate, especially for the minor characters. I didn’t take the time to double check the numbers for main characters with 10 or more appearances.

ALPHABETICAL NAME: The character’s name for alphabetical listing. Items such as “Lord” or “The” appear after their unique name.

FULL NAME: Their full name, which may or may not include a title.

FIRST: Their first name, or their title (such as “Mistress”) if their first name is uncertain or unknown. Some names such as “Mistress X” or “Master X” weren’t clear whether the X is the last or first name.

LAST: The last name. Can include multiple words if the character has a multi word name. The first and last name columns were originally autogenerated from the full name column, so there might be a couple discrepancies, but I spent a fair amount of time weeding them out, so they should be rare.

COMMON NAME: The name that is most commonly used for the character in the books. It seems to vary by gender and affiliation. For example, Aes Sedai almost always go by their first name, whereas Asha’man almost always go by their last (but not always). Some characters go by multiple variations, multiple times, so I tried to figure out which one is used the most. There might be some discrepancies, but they should be mostly accurate.

PRIMARY NAME: Their primary name. Used to identify the primary character for aliases and titles.

GENDER: The character’s gender. M = Male, F = Female, U = Unknown

SPECIES: Species of the character. Can be Human, Trolloc, Ogier, Horse, Cat, Dog, etc.

TIME: Time period that character lives in. Can be the following:
Current: Alive as of the beginning of New Spring (around the start of the Aiel war)
Historic: Dead by the beginning of New Spring
AoL: Died before the end of the Age of Legends

AFFILIATION: Organization or profession that they are affiliated with. Includes items such as Aes Sedai, Asha’man, Andoran Lord, Farmer, Luca’s Circus, etc. My methodology changed a couple times on this category, so the data isn’t entirely consistent. I also wasn’t sure what to do with people who had multiple affiliations (such as an Asha’man who was also a Tairen Lord) or switched affiliations through the series, so I usually just picked whatever seemed most appropriate.

AJAH: Their primary Ajah if they are an Aes Sedai.

DARK?: If they are a darkfriend or Black Ajah. This was originally just for Aes Sedai, but I later decided to make it for dark/light affiliation. As a result, many of the names have a value of “Unassigned”. Originally, I was using “No” for anyone that I wasn’t sure about, but at some point I added “Unknown” and “Maybe”, so I need to make some decisions on my methodology before I finish assigning values.

STRENGTH: Their power level if they are a channeler. I used “N/A” for non-channelers and “Unknown” if they channel but their exact power level isn’t specified. Some characters have both their current and potential strengths listed in the WoT Companion, so I tried to include both in the notes if so, but usually used their potential strength in this column; but I’m not certain I was consistent, so there may be some discrepancies.

NATIONALITY: The country or region that they are originally from. My methodology on this column changed in the middle of the project, so the data isn’t entirely consistent. At first I made a lot of assumptions, such as assigning Andor to an Innkeeper who has an Inn in Andor. Later on I realized that people move around, and I shouldn’t make those assumptions, so I started using “Unknown” a lot more. I still made some assumptions, such as assigning “Altara” to a young servant who is working in Ebou Dar since it seems likely that they come from the area. At some point I plan to go back and review the data, but it’s not a priority at this point.

BIRTH YEAR: What year the character was born. Most of these dates come from their entries in the WoT Companion. I could have made guesses for a bunch of characters based on their info, but I didn’t want to take the time or create potentially false data.

DESCRIPTION: A brief description of who the character is. Most of these were taken directly from the beginning of their WoT Companion entry.
